About Clean Technique

Company History and Background

Clean Technique is a small cleaning business.They specialize in cleaning for different types of clients this includes residential cleaning such as apartments, houses and Airbnbs to commercial cleaning services to office spaces and businesses. They offer cleaning services such as • Deep cleaning • Post-occupational cleaning • Carpet care • Upholstery care • Tailored cleaning packages for businesses

Our Mission Statement

To give our customers the best quality cleaning services available using eco-friendly cleaning products. We want to keep the world clean using the healthiest products.

Our Vision Statement

To be the leading eco-friendly cleaning brand in Gauteng, trusted in every home, Airbnb and office in Johannesburg and Pretoria.

Why Choose Us

  • Eco-Friendly & Safe: Non-toxic products safe for kids and pets.
  • Reliable & Professional: Trained staff, on-time service.
  • Tailored Packages: Custom solutions for homes and businesses.

Our Values & Professional Standards

Integrity, Quality, Sustainability and Customer Satisfaction. Every team member is vetted, trained and committed to leaving your space cleaner than we found it.
